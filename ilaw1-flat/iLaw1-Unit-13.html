10How does Florida’s CFO protect policyowners from IMPAIRED Insurersp.116

CFO is declared RECEIVER –

When an INSURER Audit identifies inadequate financial reserves, Florida’s OIR will immediately REVOKE their Insurer’s Certificate of Authority. This prevents more policies from being sold.

NEXT, Florida’s CFO is declared receiver over the impaired / insolvent insurers. Accounts receivable, payable and daily operations are managed by Florida’s DFS Division of Rehabilitation & Liquidation.

[ Identify ] The word IMPAIRED refers to an insurer in a Pre-Bankruptcy status. Insolvent identifies a BANKRUPT insurer.
