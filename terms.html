Text Messaging Terms

Effective November 25, 2026

These terms cover the text message program run by The Insurance School Inc. ("The Insurance School," "we," "us").

The program

The Insurance School sends text messages to students and licensed agents about their courses: class and orientation schedules, course paperwork, continuing education due date reminders, replies to questions you send us, and School news, newsletter updates, and course offers.

How to sign up

Text SCHOOL to 850-801-3100. You will get a text confirming you are signed up. Signing up for texts is not a condition of buying any course.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies with your courses and deadlines.

Cost

Msg & data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

How to stop

Reply STOP to any message. You will get one confirmation text, and then no more messages unless you sign up again.

How to get help

Reply HELP to any message, or call or text 850-801-3100.

Carriers

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

We do not share mobile information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy.

Changes to these terms

If we change these terms, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
