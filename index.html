Questions | Before You Enroll
?How do I apply for a license?
Your State Application is $55 — $50 plus $5 for your Florida license ID card — paid directly to the Florida Department of Financial Services.
?Where do I go for fingerprints?
Fingerprinting is a required background check. It typically runs $62–$75 depending on provider, paid at your fingerprint appointment.
?How do I schedule my exam?
Through Pearson VUE. The State Exam Fee is $44 per attempt, paid when you schedule.
?Is any of the $174 paid to The Insurance School?
No. Every state licensing fee is paid to the state or its vendors — never to us.
?Will a timer track my study hours?
No. iFLASH4u courses are Authorized Correspondence Courses — NO TIMER REQUIREMENT. Succeed at your pace.
?Which license should I start with?
The 2-15 bundles Health, Life & Annuities in ONLY 60 hours — saving you 10 hours versus taking the 2-14 and 2-40 separately.
?What comes with my course?
1,000+ practice questions and 6 months of access.
