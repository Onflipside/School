Financial Services Regulation
11What responsibility is given to Florida’s CFO when DOMESTIC Insurers are declared INSOLVENT?p.132

Impaired INSOLVENT

= PRE-Bankruptcy = BANKRUPT

When insurers are declared INSOLVENT, the CFO is named LIQUIDATOR. As liquidator, Florida’s CFO is responsible for selling insurer assets to pay policyowner claims and refunds for prepaid premiums.

[ Identify ] Policyowners are creditors when claims and refunds are owed.

12What is our CFO’s role when FOREIGN Insurers are declared INSOLVENT?p.133

[ iFlash4u ] FOREIGN Insurer = Insurance company NOT incorporated in Florida. Foreign Insurance companies are incorporated inside of the United States.

When a FOREIGN Insurer is declared Impaired ( OR ) Insolvent, the CFO of the foreign insurers home state becomes the LIQUIDATOR. Florida’s CFO becomes the CONSERVATOR.

As CONSERVATOR, the Florida CFO can protect the claims ( Enforceable Promises ) of Florida based policyowners.

65WHEN would an insurer be REHABILITATED? = PRE-Bankruptcyp.132

When an Office of Insurance Regulations Audit discovers a Financially IMPAIRED Insurer. Once identified, the CFO is immediately named the REHABILATATOR (AND) becomes the recipient of all accounts receivable & payable.

[ Note ] Policyowners with active claims (AND) pre-paid premiums become CREDITORS of the insurer.

81WHAT type of insurer can NOT be audited by Florida’s OIR?p.133

State regulators CAN NOT audit the activities of Alien insurers which have been conducted OUTSIDE of the United States.

PROCESS Agent Authority is granted to the Florida CFO and flows through the CFO to his APPOINTED Commissioners & Investigators. Their investigative powers END at the United States border.

126What is the role of a REHABILITATOR?p.132

As REHABILITATOR, Florida’s ELECTED Chief Financial Officer manages the accounts receivable & payable for IMPAIRED Insurers. As Rehabilitator, the CFO is responsible for helping them achieve financial stability.

127What is the purpose of a LIQUIDATOR?p.132

The CFO’s role as Liquidator oversees the sale of an INSOLVENT Insurer Assets to pay policyowner claims.

When a DOMESTIC Insurer is declared INSOLVENT by Florida’s Office of Insurance Regulations, our ELECTED Chief Financial Officer is named LIQUIDATOR by the local circuit courts.

128WHAT is the role of a CONSERVATOR & when are they named?p.134

Florida’s ELECTED Chief Financial Officer is named CONSERVATOR when a FOREIGN (OR) ALIEN Insurer is declared Impaired (OR) Insolvent.

129WHAT is the difference between an Impaired and Insolvent Insurer?p.132

[ Define ] Impaired = Pre-Bankruptcy

(VS) Insolvent = BANKRUPT

130WHO is responsible for auditing the financial soundness of FOREIGN insurers?p.133

The Office of Insurance Regulations of the state the FOREIGN Insurer is incorporated in conducts the Audit. Florida’s OIR receives a copy of the audit.
