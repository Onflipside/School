2WHAT is Quasi-Legislative Authority?p.27

Quasi = NOT Absolute,

3WHAT can Florida’s CFO NOT DO with Quasi-Judicial Authority?p.27

Chief Financial Officer CAN NOT impose INCARCERATION as a penalty. CFO can enforce Consent Orders, license Revocations and impose Restitution.
