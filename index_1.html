FLORIDA | Insurance Licensing Fees

No surprise or extra expenses when The Insurance School is on your side.

STATE APPLICATION
$55

$50 application fee + $5 for your Florida license ID card. Paid directly to the Florida Department of Financial Services.

FINGERPRINTING
~$75

Required background check. Typically runs $62-$75 depending on provider. Paid at your fingerprint appointment.

STATE EXAM FEE
$44

Per exam attempt. Paid to Pearson VUE when scheduling your Florida state licensing examination.

*NOT Paid to The Insurance School
