Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

This policy explains what The Insurance School Inc. ("The Insurance School," "we," "us") collects through TheInsuranceSchool.com and through our text messages, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • What you give us: your name, phone number, email address, and the course or event you ask about or enroll in.
  • Course records: enrollment, attendance, and completion information for the courses you take with us.
  • Messages: texts, calls, and emails you send us, and our replies.
  • Payments: card payments are handled by our payment processor.

How we use it

  • To enroll you, deliver your course, and keep your course records.
  • To answer your questions and send reminders about classes, orientation, course paperwork, and continuing education due dates.
  • To send School news, newsletter updates, and course offers to people who have agreed to receive them.

Text messaging

If you text SCHOOL to 850-801-3100, you agree to receive text messages from The Insurance School about your courses, class reminders, and School updates. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to opt out at any time.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Our text messaging provider and wireless carriers handle your number only to deliver our messages to you.

See our Text Messaging Terms for the full program details.

When we share information

  • With service providers who help us run the School, such as payment, email, and text delivery providers, only so they can do that work for us.
  • When the law or a regulator requires it.

We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop text messages.
  • Use the unsubscribe link in any email to stop marketing emails.
  • Call or text 850-801-3100 to ask what we have on file for you, or to ask us to correct or remove it. Some course records must be kept as required by law.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
